Circle of Fifths

Clockwise moves up by perfect fifths. Counter-clockwise moves by fourths. Adjacent keys share most notes.

Fast progression map

Pick any key on the wheel and use neighboring chords for smooth movement.

  • I-IV-V: center key, one counter-clockwise, one clockwise.
  • ii-V-I: minor chord one clockwise from IV, then V, then I.
  • Relative minor: use the inner minor key on the same spoke.
